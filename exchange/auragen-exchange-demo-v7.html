Three simple steps

Make a forecast without learning a new language.

The interface keeps the mechanics visible: what you believe, what it costs, and what settles the outcome.

01

Find a question you understand.

Browse by topic, creator, or closing date. Each card gives you the current probability before you open the details.

How to read a price

Price is the crowd’s current probability.

A Yes price of 62¢ means the market currently estimates a 62% chance of that outcome. It is a live consensus, not a guarantee.

Questions people ask first

Do I need to understand trading?

No. Start with the question and probability. The order panel shows your exact cost and maximum payout before anything is confirmed.

Who decides the outcome?

The market’s published resolution source decides it. AuraGen shows that source and the full criteria before trading begins.

Can anyone publish a market immediately?

No. People can propose markets, but AuraGen reviews clarity, demand, timing, and resolution rules before opening one for trading.
